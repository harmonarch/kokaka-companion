## Design Context

### Users
Kokaka is for emotionally sensitive, warm people who open the product when they need emotional support.

Their main jobs are to practice expression, talk through what they are feeling, organize emotions, receive companionship, and preserve long-term emotional state over time.

### Brand Personality
The brand personality is: sunlight, ocean, breeze.

Kokaka should feel light, close, warm, and emotionally available. It should support users without pressure, judgment, performance, or a clinical tone.

### Aesthetic Direction
The interface should support both light mode and dark mode.

Light mode should feel sunlit, quiet, and breathable, with warm natural surfaces and ocean-informed accents. Dark mode should feel calm and companionable, suitable for emotionally sensitive moments at night, without becoming heavy, neon, or overly technical.

There are no fixed reference products or anti-references yet. The current prototype already points toward a low-stimulation companion interface: warm off-white surfaces, deep green accents, compact rounded controls, and restrained chat-first composition.

### Design Principles
- Keep the first impression emotionally safe: soft contrast, clear hierarchy, and no visual pressure.
- Make chat feel like a place to express, not a tool to operate.
- Use sunlight, ocean, and breeze as material cues: gentle warmth, open space, fresh color, and quiet movement.
- Treat memory and long-term state as reassuring continuity, not surveillance or analytics.
- Support both daytime and nighttime emotional use with equally considered light and dark themes.
